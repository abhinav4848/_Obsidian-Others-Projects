# 2023-08-01 Patreon letter - Initial experiments in self-explanation support
_Summary of the source letter._

The letter distinguishes a representation of a text's literal statements from an understanding connected to prior knowledge and inferred relationships. Self-explanation can strengthen those connections and reveal comprehension gaps. Matuschak experiments with passage-linked explanations, visual feedback, and language-model feedback, but finds that compulsory explanation can feel burdensome. He seeks reading interactions that encourage effective comprehension strategies while producing something the reader values beyond the immediate exercise.
