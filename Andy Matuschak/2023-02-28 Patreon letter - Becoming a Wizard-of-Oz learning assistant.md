# 2023-02-28 Patreon letter - Becoming a Wizard-of-Oz learning assistant
_Summary of the source letter._

Matuschak becomes a learning assistant for an adult pursuing a demanding physics project. Tutoring recordings and narrated review sessions reveal confusion, motivation, and weaknesses that interviews alone had missed. He writes and revises prompts in response, connecting them to the learner's meaningful experiences. The experiment explores close collaboration as a way to discover better learning tools, with practical feedback shaping both the prompts and the surrounding system.
