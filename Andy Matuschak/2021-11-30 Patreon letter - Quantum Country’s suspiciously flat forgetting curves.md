# 2021-11-30 Patreon letter - Quantum Country’s suspiciously flat forgetting curves
_Summary of the source letter._

Quantum Country experiments show unexpectedly little decline in recall across some delays, making conventional forgetting-curve interpretations difficult. Matuschak investigates differences among questions and considers whether the wording of prompts supplies cues that inflate apparent retention. He cautions that these preliminary results may not generalize to other memory systems. The central research problem is explaining the measurements before using them to justify changes in review scheduling.
