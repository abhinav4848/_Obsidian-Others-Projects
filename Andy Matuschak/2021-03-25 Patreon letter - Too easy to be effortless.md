# 2021-03-25 Patreon letter - Too easy to be effortless
_Summary of the source letter._

A memory system can achieve high recall while still making practice feel tedious. Matuschak distinguishes a feeling of effortless engagement from tasks that are merely easy. Quantum Country's often trivial reviews motivate questions about boredom, appropriate challenge, and the emotional experience of maintenance practice. Scheduling and other optimizations matter insofar as they help the system feel more closely connected to the learner's intentions.
