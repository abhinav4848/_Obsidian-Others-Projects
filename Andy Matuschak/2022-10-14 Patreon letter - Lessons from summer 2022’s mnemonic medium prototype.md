# 2022-10-14 Patreon letter - Lessons from summer 2022’s mnemonic medium prototype
_Summary of the source letter._

Observing a new mnemonic-medium prototype reveals how much outcomes depend on readers' aims and prior familiarity with spaced repetition. Margin prompts can guide attention, but they also encourage distracted or compulsory prompt-reading. Some readers want to mark an important idea without immediately committing to repeated practice. Matuschak explores more flexible reading interactions, distinguishing attention, expression, and tentative interest from the stronger decision to save a memory prompt.
