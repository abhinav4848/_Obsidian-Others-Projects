# 2021-08-24 Patreon letter - Revamping the mnemonic medium around reader control
_Summary of the source letter._

Early mnemonic texts make the author's chosen prompts dominate the reading experience. Matuschak argues that readers need more fundamental control over what they study, how they express it, and which personal connections they record. Editing or deleting assigned prompts alone does not resolve the underlying imbalance. He proposes treating author-written prompts as adaptable material within the reader's own learning practice, with fluid opportunities to select and create.
