# 2022-12-27 Patreon letter - Towards impact through intimacy in my memory system research
_Summary of the source letter._

Matuschak redirects his memory-system research toward the depth and usefulness of understanding. Broad adoption and improved recall scores do not by themselves establish that people can use what they learn. He proposes close work with a few committed learners, including coaching, diaries, and bespoke interventions. The aim is to observe cognitive and emotional changes in detail and discover promising design principles before pursuing broader evaluation or scale.
