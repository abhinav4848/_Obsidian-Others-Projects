# 2020-09-30 Patreon letter - The carrying capacity of a regular memory practice; deliberate practice and flow
_Summary of the source letter._

A regular memory practice could gradually support a substantial body of knowledge, but review capacity is only part of the problem. Writing effective prompts about conceptual material requires considerable skill and effort. Matuschak explores how expert-written prompts embedded in explanations might reduce that burden while retaining meaningful context. The value of the practice depends on reinforcing ideas that matter to the learner rather than accumulating disconnected trivia.
