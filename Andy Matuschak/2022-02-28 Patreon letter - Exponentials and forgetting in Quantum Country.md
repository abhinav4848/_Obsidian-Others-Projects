# 2022-02-28 Patreon letter - Exponentials and forgetting in Quantum Country
_Summary of the source letter._

The letter revisits Quantum Country's memory data and the possibility that additional practice yields large increases in retention time. It separates durable recall, review cost, forgetting without practice, and transfer into real-world understanding as different questions. Unexpectedly slow forgetting complicates interpretation of the experiments. Matuschak presents the analysis as preliminary and emphasizes that improved recall alone does not establish deeper understanding or practical ability.
